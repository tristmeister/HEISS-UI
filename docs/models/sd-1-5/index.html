How to run SD 1.5 locally.

SD 1.5 runs in ComfyUI from one 2.1 GB checkpoint, the same file ComfyUI’s default workflow loads. It needs about 4 GB of video memory and makes 512 × 512 images. Runway deleted the original download in 2024; the file lives on in Comfy-Org’s archive.

Still worth running.

Stable Diffusion 1.5 came out in October 2022, made by RunwayML and CompVis with Stability’s compute. It’s a model of about 0.9B parameters with one text encoder, CLIP ViT-L, trained at 512 × 512.

It’s small and quick, it runs on almost any graphics card from the last few years, and it has years of fine-tunes, LoRAs and ControlNets behind it, including anime models like Anything, Counterfeit and MeinaMix. Its model card is honest about the limits: no legible text, weak faces and complex scenes, and English prompts only.

For new work on 8 GB or more, SDXL gives better images at 1024 pixels. SD 1.5 is the pick for 4 to 6 GB cards, old laptops, huge batches, or a fine-tune and LoRA set you already rely on.

Where did SD 1.5 go?

Runway deleted runwayml/stable-diffusion-v1-5 from Hugging Face in August 2024, which broke many download links. The same files are in Comfy-Org’s archive and in the stable-diffusion-v1-5 mirror.

Files you need.

One checkpoint, which holds the model, the text encoder and a VAE. A better VAE is optional and small.

  • Checkpoint v1-5-pruned-emaonly-fp16.safetensors ComfyUI/models/checkpoints/ or v1-5-pruned-emaonly.safetensors, fp32, 4.3 GB
    2.1 GB Download
  • VAE vae-ft-mse-840000-ema-pruned.safetensors ComfyUI/models/vae/ optional: cleaner colours and faces than the built-in VAE
    0.3 GB Download

The fp16 file makes practically the same images as the fp32 one at half the download. The 7.7 GB v1-5-pruned.safetensors carries extra weights for training and isn’t needed to make images. Fine-tunes from Civitai go in the same folder and load the same way.

What fits your computer.

A 512 × 512 image at 20 to 25 steps. Comfy estimates its default SD 1.5 workflow at 3.1 GB of video memory.

  • 4 GBTight

    Runs. ComfyUI’s own guide puts SD 1.5 at about 4 GB.

  • 6 GBFits

    Runs smoothly. A GTX 1660 Super makes a 512 image in under 20 seconds.

  • 8 GBFits

    Room for LoRAs, ControlNets, hires passes and batches.

  • 12 GB+Fits

    Large batches. At this size SDXL is the better model.

  • Mac 8 GB+Fits

    The 2.1 GB file runs on any Apple Silicon Mac. The lightest way to try ComfyUI on one.

GTX 16 series

GTX 1650 and 1660 cards run in fp32 in ComfyUI, since fp16 is unreliable on them. SD 1.5 is small enough that this still works well.

Set it up.

  1. 1

    Open the default workflow

    A fresh ComfyUI opens with an SD 1.5 workflow. In the template browser it’s Image Generation. It expects v1-5-pruned-emaonly-fp16.safetensors.

  2. 2

    Download the checkpoint

    Put the file in ComfyUI/models/checkpoints, then press R or restart ComfyUI so Load Checkpoint lists it.

  3. 3

    Add the better VAE

    Optional. Add a Load VAE node with vae-ft-mse-840000-ema-pruned and connect it to VAE Decode instead of the checkpoint’s VAE output.

  4. 4

    For anime fine-tunes: clip skip

    Models from the NovelAI line (Anything, Counterfeit, MeinaMix and their merges) expect clip skip 2. Put CLIP Set Last Layer at -2 between the checkpoint and the prompt nodes.

  5. 5

    Run

    Keep the size at 512 × 512 or close to it, such as 512 × 768. For bigger images, generate at 512 and upscale, or send the result through a second KSampler at partial denoise.

Comfy’s default template uses 20 steps, CFG 8, euler and normal. dpmpp_2m with karras at CFG 7 is the other common default. SD 1.5 has no official recommendation beyond that, and fine-tunes often list their own on their download page.

Fewer steps

Speed LoRAs exist for SD 1.5 too, from LCM, Hyper-SD and TCD. Hyper-SD’s SD 1.5 LoRAs run at the step count in their name with ddim and sgm_uniform at CFG 1, like the SDXL ones. The Hyper-SD15-8steps-CFG-lora keeps a real CFG, so negative prompts still work.

When it goes wrong.

Value not in list: ckpt_name: 'v1-5-pruned-emaonly-fp16.safetensors' not in []
The most common first-run error. The default workflow needs the checkpoint in models/checkpoints. Download it, then press R.
Faded, washed-out colours
The VAE inside the checkpoint. Load vae-ft-mse-840000-ema-pruned with a Load VAE node.
Doubled people and repeated objects at large sizes
SD 1.5 was trained at 512 and, as Comfy’s template notes, doesn’t do well at large sizes. Generate at 512 and upscale.
lora key not loaded
The LoRA is for SDXL or another model. SD 1.5 only takes SD 1.5 LoRAs.
mat1 and mat2 shapes cannot be multiplied with a ControlNet
The ControlNet is for SDXL. Use an SD 1.5 ControlNet.
Unsupported head_dim: 160 with --use-sage-attention
SageAttention doesn’t handle one of SD 1.5’s layer sizes. Start ComfyUI without that flag for SD 1.5.

Questions.

Where can I download SD 1.5 now?

From Comfy-Org’s archive on Hugging Face (v1-5-pruned-emaonly-fp16.safetensors, 2.1 GB) or the stable-diffusion-v1-5 mirror. Runway deleted its original repo in August 2024.

How much VRAM does SD 1.5 need?

About 4 GB, and it runs smoothly on 6 GB cards. Comfy estimates its default workflow at 3.1 GB.

Is SD 1.5 still worth using in 2026?

On 4 to 6 GB cards, yes, and when you rely on its fine-tunes, LoRAs or ControlNets. With 8 GB or more, SDXL makes better images at 1024 pixels.

Which VAE should I use with SD 1.5?

vae-ft-mse-840000-ema-pruned.safetensors from Stability, loaded with a Load VAE node. It gives cleaner colours and faces than the VAE inside most checkpoints.

Do SDXL LoRAs work on SD 1.5?

No. SD 1.5 and SDXL are different architectures. The LoRA fails with lora key not loaded and changes nothing.

Why do my SD 1.5 images look wrong at 1024 pixels?

It was trained at 512 × 512, so large sizes repeat subjects and break anatomy. Generate at 512, or 512 × 768, and upscale.

HEISS UI

Small model, same studio.

HEISS UI runs SD 1.5 and its fine-tunes on the ComfyUI you already have, next to your newer models.

  • Anime fine-tunes set up right. Anime checkpoints get the setting they were trained with, from the file name.
  • Your LoRAs, sorted. The ones made for this model come first, with their trigger words.
  • Drop in the file and it runs. It knows the model from the file itself, even renamed, and picks settings that work.
  • Upscale and compare. One click to upscale with SeedVR2, then drag a slider to see what changed.
